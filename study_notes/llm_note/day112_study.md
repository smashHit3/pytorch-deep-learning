# Day 112: Write a final summary of what you learned and what to study next.

## Goal
Turn completed exercises into an evidence-based LLM learning retrospective and a prioritized next-study plan.

## Today's Focus
Summarize the concepts and artifacts you actually completed, identify gaps without treating toy results as mastery, and choose concrete next steps.

## Study Tasks / Hands-on Exercise
1. Run `python study_notes/llm_daily/day112_study.py` and replace its example milestones, evidence, and gaps with your own.
2. Write three claims about what you learned and attach one exercise, output, or note that supports each claim.
3. List the most important gaps in your knowledge or portfolio, then rank the next three study actions by expected value.
4. Record one limitation of your retrospective and one criterion that would show progress on the next action.

## Key Concepts
evidence-based reflection, milestones, limitations, skill gaps, prioritized next steps.

## Mini Challenges
- Distinguish a completed exercise from demonstrated mastery.
- Name one artifact you can show and one capability you still need to validate.
- Make one next-step goal measurable enough to check in a week.
